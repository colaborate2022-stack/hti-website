Salt is like training – the presence is unnoticed; the absence – ruins organisations.

Solid product, fancy office, snazzy website & social media make an impact; but it’s your team that makes or breaks your brand! Dream teams convert one-off patrons into loyal customers; raving fans. Dream teams are not found 'readymade'; they are intentionally built by persistent training that leads to organisational growth & expansion.

Growth and expansion calls for hiring and promoting. New hires must be trained & when promoting, both incumbent and replacement must be trained to handle the next level. Training is irreplaceable – like salt.
