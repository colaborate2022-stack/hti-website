Questions people ask before filling this in

How long does the survey take?

About three minutes. It's 15 short questions about your team, the skill gaps you're seeing and when you'd like to start. Nothing needs to be looked up in advance.

What do I get back?

An HTI training consultant reads your answers and comes back within one working day with the programs that match your team, an outline of what each session covers, and an indicative schedule built around your shifts.

Is it free? Am I committing to anything?

The survey and the recommendation are free, and there's no obligation to book. Plenty of teams use it simply to understand where their gaps are.

Who is this designed for?

Hotels and resorts, restaurants, QSRs and cloud kitchens, and corporate office and retail support teams. We've trained over 300,000 participants across 410+ cities in 13+ languages — from a single outlet to national chains.
