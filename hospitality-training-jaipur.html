Training for the way Jaipur actually works

Jaipur sells an experience that its staff have to deliver in person, every time, to guests who have paid specifically for it. Heritage and palace properties compete on a kind of service that cannot be improvised, and much of it is carried by long-serving staff who learned it by apprenticeship. The destination-wedding trade layers on a second pattern entirely — enormous, short, high-stakes events where a property triples its floor with temporary hands who have to look and sound like the rest of the team by day one. Both are the same problem underneath: the standard lives in people’s heads and not in a programme. Hotel SHOT, FORT and HOT-CAR are where most Jaipur properties start.

HTI is headquartered in Navi Mumbai and the trainers travel. There is no Jaipur branch office and this page does not pretend otherwise: what comes to you is the trainer and the programme, delivered at your own property.

How a Jaipur booking runs

  1. You tell us what is going wrong. Covers dropping, reviews slipping, a new outlet opening, a team that has never been trained. A consultant calls back within one working day.
  2. We agree the programme, dates and language. Group size, which shifts, and which of the programmes above fits the roles you need fixed.
  3. The trainer comes to you. Sessions run at your property in Jaipur, scheduled around service rather than across it.
  4. You get a report. What was covered, who took part, and what the floor still needs — so the next round is not a guess.

Questions about training in Jaipur

Do you have an office in Jaipur?

No. HTI is headquartered at Vashi, Navi Mumbai, and the trainers travel to you. The training itself runs at your property in Jaipur — on your floor, with your equipment and your menu — which is where it works best in any case.

Where does the training actually happen?

At your own hotel, restaurant, outlet or office in Jaipur. HTI does not run open-enrolment batches in a rented classroom; a session is booked by one employer for one team, and it is built around the situations that team meets on its own shift.

Which language will the session be in?

Tell us which language your floor staff work in and we will match a trainer to it. HTI has run sessions in 13 languages across 410+ cities; in Jaipur the request is usually Hindi and English. Training somebody in a language they think in and not merely understand is most of the difference between a session that changes a shift and one that fills an afternoon.

Which programme suits a business in Jaipur?

It depends on the roles you are trying to fix rather than on the city. Of the 8 listed on this page, Hotel SHOT, FORT and HOT-CAR are the ones Jaipur employers most often start with. If you are not sure, say what is going wrong on the floor and a consultant will point you at the right one.
