Food Safety Training

FSSAI Food Safety Training

FSSAI requires every food business to have a trained Food Safety Supervisor. But the supervisor is not the person plating the food. We train the whole team that handles it — on your premises, in the languages they work in.

Your supervisor is certified. Your kitchen is only as safe as the person handling the food.

Who it’s for

Every kitchen fails differently

A cloud kitchen loses control after the food leaves. A caterer loses it in transit. The session is built around where your operation is actually exposed.

Restaurants & QSRs

High-volume kitchens where crews change often and every new joiner needs the standard fast.

Hotels

Outlets, banquets and room service off shared kitchens, where one weak link affects every cover.

Cloud kitchens

Food sits in transit after it leaves you — packing and holding decide what the customer opens.

Catering

Cooked in one place, transported, held and served hours later under far less control.

Food manufacturing & retail

Production units, packhouses and store teams, with audit trails and documentation to maintain.

The Curriculum

What the training covers

  • 01
    Food safety fundamentalsBiological, chemical and physical hazards, how contamination spreads, and the few controls that prevent most of it.
  • 02
    Personal and kitchen hygieneHandwashing at the moments that matter, grooming standards, reporting illness and cuts, and the habits behind a genuinely clean kitchen.
  • 03
    Storage and temperature controlChecking deliveries, store organisation, stock rotation, and safe cooking, cooling, holding and reheating temperatures.
  • 04
    Cross-contaminationSeparating raw and ready-to-eat food, colour-coded equipment, allergen handling, and where a busy service goes wrong.
  • 05
    Cleaning and sanitationCleaning versus sanitising, correct dilution and contact times, schedules that survive a rush, waste and pest control.
  • 06
    Documentation and record-keepingTemperature logs, cleaning checklists and traceability records — useful during service, defensible during an inspection.
