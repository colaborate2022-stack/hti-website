The case for it

Why SOPs?

  • 01
    Systems form an idealThey are a perfect step-by-step process of what is to be. When companies are formed, they are formed with a vision. As the processes align with the vision, you might find yourself lucky enough to find the perfect person for the job.
  • 02
    Sounds great? Until it's notIt is great because they are the perfect person. It is not great because what happens when you want to expand? One person can only do so much. And what if they leave?
  • 03
    Your star players can do bigger workThe star players of your team can be put to better use — bigger use.
  • 04
    So break the job downHave them break down their tasks (Standard Operating Procedures — SOPs) in fine detail, in a way that anyone can replicate those tasks.
  • 05
    Documented means transferableOnce the work is written down, it moves with the role instead of walking out with the person.
  • 06
    New staff get productive soonerThe training period required for a new staffer taking up that job role can be drastically decreased, and with the help of daily checklists and step-by-step processes, their work can be made delay-free.

Why it matters

One person's know-how, made repeatable by everyone

Every growing team has someone who simply knows how it is done. That knowledge is the most valuable thing in the building, and the most fragile — it sits with one person, and it leaves when they do.

Writing it down is what turns it into an asset the business owns. SOPs allow teammates with limited training or instruction to replicate tasks consistently, which is what makes Speed, Scale and Skilling possible.
